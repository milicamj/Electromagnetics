For a point charge $Q$ above an infinite grounded conducting plane, keep the real charge and replace the conductor with an image charge $-Q$ at the mirror position below the plane, as shown in Figure \ref{fig:imageMethod}. The two-charge potential is zero on the plane and reproduces the potential and field only in the physical region above it. The image is a mathematical construction; the actual electric field inside the conductor is zero in electrostatic equilibrium.
